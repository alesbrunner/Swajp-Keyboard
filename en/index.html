The keyboard that
types with a swipe

Swajp is an Android keyboard inspired by the beloved Swype. Glide your finger across the letters, it guesses words from context – and everything stays on your phone.

Coming soon to Google PlayLanguage packs

Features

Swipe typing

Glide over the letters and the word appears. The keyboard gradually learns your own swipes.

Gestures

Copy, paste, undo, select all – one swipe from the Swajp key, like Ctrl on a computer.

Text editing

Select by words, lines or paragraphs, arrow keys, jump to start or end, change case with one swipe.

Two languages at once

Type in two languages without switching – the keyboard knows words from both. 27 languages in total.

Offline dictation

Speech to text right on your phone (Parakeet, Omnilingual) or via Google. Words are checked against the dictionary.

Clipboard history

Your last 30 copied texts at hand. Passwords and sensitive data are never stored.

Number pad & calculator

Big digits for quick entry, a calculator and a scientific calculator right in the keyboard.

Privacy

No internet access, no ads, no tracking. Nothing is learned or stored in password fields.
